THE 30-SECOND JUDGE ANSWER

What does the quantum part do?

It searches a compact route-choice problem built from terrain-aware legs.

01

Classical baseline

A* searches the full cell grid and returns the lowest-cost legal route for the selected weights. It is the optimal reference for this terrain model.

02

Quantum formulation

The QAOA side first builds a small waypoint graph. Each possible directed leg gets a binary choice; the QUBO cost rewards inexpensive legs and penalizes broken flow at the start, goal, or intermediate waypoints.

03

Honest comparison

QAOA samples candidate bitstrings, then the best valid waypoint route is expanded back onto the grid and rescored. It is a heuristic over a smaller graph, so it can take a longer route. This demo makes no quantum-speedup claim.
